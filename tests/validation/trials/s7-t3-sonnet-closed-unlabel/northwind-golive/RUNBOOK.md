### What was changed, and why

Everything Northwind-specific is in `configs/northwind.yaml`. The service defaults (`configs/defaults.yaml`) and
the other clients' configs are untouched; every new code path is switched on by a Northwind config key and the
existing clients' schedules and eval files were re-run for all their committed weeks and are byte-identical.

- **Ingest** (`wfm/ingest/loader.py`). The export's `utc_offset` is now authoritative: UTC is built from the label plus the
  row's own offset. Localizing the label cannot separate the repeated 01:00–02:00 hour of the fall-back day
  (2026-11-01, so 2026-W44 has 169 h = 338 intervals). The grid is the ISO week itself (Monday 00:00 to Monday 00:00
  local), so DST weeks have 334 or 338 intervals and a missing row is zero volume, with a warning. The queue list comes from
  the config (it was hard-wired to priority and standard, which silently dropped `vip`). Rows labelled
  a minute off the grid (see "Data" below) are folded into the 30-minute interval they belong to.
- **Staffing** (`wfm/staffing/required.py`). The occupancy cap is applied (`enforce_occupancy_cap`), required staffing uses
  each interval's own handle time (`aht_smoothing: interval`; the code also averaged it away in that mode) and Erlang C
  without abandonment (`queueing: erlang`). SLA targets are the SOW's, including `vip`. Outside a queue's operating
  hours (`queue_hours`, vip 08:00–20:00) the requirement is zero.
- **Scheduling** (`wfm/scheduler/greedy.py`, new `wfm/scheduler/search.py`). The rest rule (11 h elapsed, `min_rest_hours`),
  split shifts for PT only, elapsed-hour weekly caps and a vip floor of one agent 06:00–24:00 (`min_cover`, no cross-skill
  credit) are enforced while placing shifts. Days off are spread over the week before shifts are placed
  (`days_off_plan`), because a day-by-day fill leaves nobody for the weekend once the few vip agents have worked five days.
  `search.py` then improves the week with a fixed-seed local search whose cost is the real post-break shortfall plus
  scheduled hours, bounded by 1.57 × required hours. Breaks follow SOW §2.3 (20 minutes, 2–5 h after the shift start, in the
  interval with the largest gross surplus) and are recomputed for every candidate schedule. The unfinished draft
  `greedy_v2.py` was not used (its rest test is inverted: it accepts shifts that are too close together).
- **Evaluation and reporting** (`wfm/evaluate/metrics.py`, `wfm/report/runbook.py`). For Northwind, attainment is the fraction of
  operating intervals where scheduled agents net of breaks meet the requirement (`attainment_basis: service_level`,
  `attainment_denominator: operating`), and the audit also counts rest, split-shift and break violations. `RUNBOOK.md` and
  `exceptions.md` are written from the run's own `eval.json`.
- **Registry and docs.** `clients/registry.yaml`, `clients/README.md`, the changelog and the architecture notes.

### SOW §2.8 — pre-existing deviations from the §1 method that affect Northwind's queues

These are how the service behaved before this change. None of them is still in effect for Northwind; they still apply to
clients on the defaults.

1. WFM-212: `occupancy_cap` was read but never applied in `staffing/required.py` (§1.3). All three queues were affected.
2. WFM-198: required staffing used the queue's mean handle time, not each interval's own (§1.2), and in `interval` mode
   the code still collapsed handle time to the mean.
3. PR #47: attainment defaulted to `coverage` (scheduled ÷ required, capped at 1) instead of "interval met or not" (§1.2, §1.6),
   and the evaluator left zero-volume intervals out of the denominator instead of counting every operating interval.
4. The loader only knew the queues priority and standard, so `vip` demand was discarded; the defaults carry no `vip` target.
5. Local-time export: the label was localized with the client's timezone and the `utc_offset` column was ignored, wrong
   for the repeated hour on a fall-back day; the week grid was whatever rows arrived rather than the ISO week.
6. `min_cover` credited an agent on another queue with 0.5 toward a floor (`cross_skill_weight`); §2.6 and §1.2 count an agent
   once, on the queue they work.
7. Scheduling: breaks were placed at the shift midpoint (§2.3 asks for the largest-surplus interval), there was no rest
   rule (§2.1), and split templates were open to every contract (§2.2).

The Erlang C routine in `wfm/staffing/erlang.py` matches §1.2 (checked against an independent implementation). `vendor/qlib`
(abandonment) is Fabrikam's and is not used for Northwind.

### Outcome and open items (SOW §2.7)

- `priority` and `standard` meet their targets with room to spare in the weeks supplied (2026-W42 to W44).
- `vip` does not reach 0.95 weekly attainment with this roster (about 0.90–0.92). Only 11 agents hold `vip`, all full-time, so
  vip cover is capped at 11 × 40 h = 440 h a week, and the schedule uses it (436–438 h). Relaxing the rest rule or the
  five-day limit does not change the result; the weekly-hour cap is what binds. This is recorded in `exceptions.md`
  for the account team: more vip-skilled agents (or hours) are needed to close the gap.
- Total scheduled hours stay under the §1.6 bound of 1.57 × required hours (about 1.47–1.49 ×).
- The search is fixed-seed and deterministic; a run takes about 15 seconds. `search_iterations` and `search_seed` in the config tune it.
